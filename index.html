📜 HOW TO PLAY

🕵️ Your Mission: Track down the mastermind behind the Whitby Lighthouse conspiracy. Interview suspects, solve puzzles, and collect evidence to unlock the truth.

📁 Case File: Review detective notes, interview transcripts, and important documents. The cryptogram becomes available after solving all main puzzles.

🗂️ Interviews: Ask suspects questions to uncover inconsistencies. Each correct answer moves you closer to the truth.

🧩 Main Puzzles: Solve location-based riddles to reveal encoded digits. These digits unlock the final cryptogram.

🎁 Bonus Puzzles: Optional challenges that provide additional clues and unlock more interview questions.

🌐 Using External Resources: Some puzzles require real-world knowledge. Feel free to use Google Maps, Wikipedia, or any online search to help solve the clues. A detective uses every tool available!

💡 Hints: Use the hint button if you're stuck – each hint costs 2 prison years and 1 hint credit.

⚖️ Accuse: Once the cryptogram is solved, the Accuse button appears. Choose the right suspect to close the case!

🔍 Good luck, detective. The truth is out there.
